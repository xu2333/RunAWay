# Can a reader use a requirement already present on a fixed card?

In the [previous study](../../docs/results/real-control-switch-v1.md) both new cards explicitly preserved the owner's cross-game goal and the assistant's conditional control proposal. The two plans read from the 575-character card specified the control switch but stopped at variation across operations. One even identified shared upper-level components. Neither said what would carry into another game and what its demonstrations would have to supply. Both plans read from the 572-character card connected a shared pipeline to game-specific skills or data.

One possible explanation is that a reader can apply an implementation detail while narrowing the owner's goal to the immediate example. The 572-character card opens with that goal and mentions its scope again after the proposed implementation. These are observed wording and output differences in two selected cards. An experiment on the reader alone cannot isolate position, phrasing or stochastic sampling as the cause.

## Frozen comparison

Keep both validated portable strings identical, including header and trailing newline. Use the previous real task's seven current messages, prompt, answer schema and model. Compare the original reader with one [additional system instruction](reader.py) that asks for a concrete consequence of relevant context at its stated scope. The instruction contains no domain example, named decision or rubric. No claim IDs, extracted profiles, original history, old answers or grades travel with the cards. No fresh extraction. The calls are two cards by two readers by two independent deliveries. Four unique static requests run twice each in the declared order. New controls using the old reader measure same-input variation. The previous study supplies descriptive history only. The live comparison does not require sending the 27 prior messages.

One owner and one previously inspected task. Request gpt-6.1-sol via the local Codex CLI, at most eight attempts with 180 seconds per attempt. Token and monetary usage are uncapped. A transport failure, invalid answer or unknown usage closes the remaining scope with no retries, repairs, recovery or replacements. Retain two planned deliveries in each cell. Usage for any failed attempt may be unknown.

## Assessment

Shuffle the eight outputs without condition labels. Lock excerpt-backed judgments before revealing which card and reader produced them. Reuse the [previous delivery criteria](../real-control-switch-v1/README.md#assessment-before-unblinding) for quality, transfer, the operational control distinction, multi-touch, explicit provenance and misattribution. A usable design must explain how to learn an executable procedure and verify success. Cross-game transfer requires a concrete common mechanism and a route for a different game to fit it. Distinguishing activities within one game alone leaves transfer unclear. The control distinction requires discrete versus continuous actions and an execution consequence. A joint pass needs quality, transfer and the control distinction in the same plan without falsely treating the proposed controller as an owner mandate or established capability. Explicitly crediting the original proposer is reported separately. Also note spurious commitments, irrelevant recital of card text and loss of qualifications in comparable answers.

Report each criterion, joint count and both-read success per card and reader. The repair target requires two jointly successful new reads on the 575-character card. To claim an observed improvement, also require more joint successes than its concurrent old reads. If the old reader already achieves two, report no demonstrated gain. The 572-character guard requires two joint passes with the new reader and no drop in joint count versus its concurrent old reads. Report any component regression, even if the joint count does not change. Missing or unclear reads cannot satisfy a required pass. If a concurrent comparison is incomplete, report it as incomplete. The two repeats provide a small functional check on fixed material, not a statistical noninferiority claim. The revised reader is an experimental candidate.
