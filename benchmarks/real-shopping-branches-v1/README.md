## What earns credit

Grade shuffled plans before opening card or method assignments. Assess television and tablet independently. For each, identify the relevant qualification, concrete qualifying route and benefit, plus a usable ordinary alternative when ineligible. Conditional verification is appropriate when the owner's eligibility is unknown. Count an unsupported claim of eligibility against that branch. A generic suggestion to check discounts or to buy elsewhere earns at most partial credit. Record whether the plan recovered the magnitude of the benefit and the specific ordinary offer as separate detail checks.

Retain the earlier requirements for task quality, exact television designation and size, confirmed bundle, usable membership-free route, and conditional third-party cashback. Also flag unsupported claims of live information. Report each branch, both in the same answer, and all seven requirements together, by card and by method. Keep missing reads in the denominator. Compare each previously successful requirement directly with the concurrent baseline. Trace each omission through the detailed decision (excluding evidence quotes), the portable text and the delivery.

Repeated complete recovery requires both branches in all four candidate deliveries, all collateral requirements on those same deliveries, and no unsupported claims. Report ties and partial gains separately. Reads of one card share an extraction. This design cannot establish generalization or actual savings.

## Execution

Use gpt-6.1-sol for up to twelve local CLI attempts, each limited to 180 seconds, without a token or monetary cap. Transport, validation or unknown usage closes the remaining slots. No retries, replacement cards, or live shopping. Only derived evidence is public.
