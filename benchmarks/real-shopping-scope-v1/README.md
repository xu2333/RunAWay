# Can a short card preserve which offers qualify for a real shopping plan?

A shopper requested a timed holiday shopping guide and advance preparation. The current user message identifies the occasion and deliverable; it gives no products, models, merchants, memberships, prices or earlier choice. The preceding conversation specified a television series and size, ruled out a membership-dependent route for a different item, and recorded a later decision to buy a particular retailer's accessory bundle. It also discussed optional benefits for other items. This task has not appeared among the repository's earlier real-task replays. It is retrospectively selected for observable selection criteria. Live shopping advice, current prices and an actual purchase are outside the task.

Use the entire available human-visible prior conversation through the preceding answer. Exclude links and machine/tool data. The original shopping guide and later messages are withheld. Historical assistant-reported prices, terms and stock are time-bound leads. The exact private rubric and user anchors are frozen before approval.

## Four concurrent conditions

Use the [existing extractor pair](../real-selection-name-extraction-v1/encoder.py) unchanged. Each extractor sees only the earlier text and independently produces two whole portable cards of at most 768 Unicode characters. Each card receives two deliveries. The same current message receives two current-only and two full-history deliveries. All twelve deliveries use the identical prior-reader system instruction, including the history controls. This is the reader used in the automatic name extraction study, before the decision-level reader intervention. Only the supplied context changes. The [builders and slot order](study.py) contain no case-specific extraction or reading instructions.

## Frozen assessment

Score shuffled deliveries with excerpts before revealing assignments. Give met, partial, absent, unclear or contradicted statuses; count only full credit. Keep missing slots in all denominators.

- **Usable task delivery.** Give advance preparation and a timed or ordered buying plan for the three intended categories. Relative times and verification steps are acceptable. A generic holiday checklist gets at most partial credit.
- **Television eligibility (primary).** Use the exact owner-specified brand/series designation and screen size to select a qualifying offer. Equivalent spellings are acceptable. An incidental name, broad product family or different size cannot establish full retention. Record name presence separately from correct operational use, in both extraction stages and answers.
- **Chosen bundle.** Carry forward the selected retailer's specific dryer with stand at its historical offer level as the plan's intended purchase, subject to checking availability. Do not silently substitute a lower-priced, materially different package. Distinguish the owner's confirmed choice from an assistant recommendation.
- **Membership.** Provide an actionable dryer route usable without the identified club membership. A valid ordinary checkout earns practical credit; separately record whether the answer explicitly identifies the named unavailable club and its scope. Do not generalize that absence to other memberships.
- **Conditional discounts.** Preserve at least one relevant optional television or tablet benefit as conditional on the corresponding membership, card or qualification, with an ordinary alternative; avoid inventing owner eligibility. Generic advice to look for coupons is partial at best.
- **Cashback.** Separate checkout cost from conditional, later third-party marketplace cashback. Give a concrete activation or offer-compatibility check. Do not present a rebate as guaranteed or as money off at checkout. Exact volatile reward rates need not be memorized.

Report each component, each card out of two, each method out of four and the conjunction on the same plan. Separately flag unsupported live checks, invented sale times presented as official, and assumed eligibility. After the assessment is locked, trace source, detailed decision (excluding evidence quotes), portable text and each delivery. Distinguish omission in generation from a reader failing to apply a retained constraint. Report history signal on the primary endpoint if both full-history reads use the qualifying television and current-only passes fewer. Call automatic primary retention repeated only if both candidate cards bind the designation and size to eligibility and all four candidate deliveries use them correctly. Compare with the original under the same standard. A tie provides no incremental evidence. Report any reduced collateral score; claim a complete repeatable repair only with the history signal and all four candidate plans passing the full conjunction without an unsupported claim.

The broad task is deliberately underspecified in isolation. A gain may reflect recovery of basic project facts. One owner, one previously unmeasured task and two generated cards per method cannot establish general personalization. No independent human acceptance is measured.

## Execution boundary

At most sixteen serial local Codex attempts on gpt-6.1-sol: four extractions and twelve deliveries. Up to 180 seconds per attempt, no token or monetary cap. A transport or validation failure or unknown usage closes remaining slots; no retries, repairs, replacements or resumption. No browsing, purchasing or retailer contact. Only anonymized derived evidence is exported.
