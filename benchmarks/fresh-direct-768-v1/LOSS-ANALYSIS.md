All sixteen cards have six declared objects, six declared aliases and eighteen active public rules. These counts describe structure, not proof of semantic coverage. Every whole card, including its caution/header/legend, is at most 768 Unicode characters.

**All sixteen actual cards use the existing dense layout. None selects the new common-condition layout.** The candidate is 15.3% shorter on the observed mean, but its request guidance also differs. Thus this experiment supports the observed source-only short-card pathway on these sources; it does not establish a causal benefit of the new grammar. Existing dictionary packing and repeated conditions already fit these records. The public replay detects the chosen syntax before expanding it; reversible restoration alone would hide that distinction.

## Why this is not the end of the fidelity question

The new people and message order are fresh, but their policy structure is highly repetitive: six projects, the same two full action names, one threshold and similar ordinary/narrow branches. Source-ID enumeration, explicit user commitments and a known rendering language may make this easier than realistic autobiographical inference. The author knew earlier development failures and also authored the reference. Hand/reference-policy consistency is not independent semantic adjudication. No person-level statistical generalization follows from these four units or their repeated reads.

A useful next challenge must vary the information itself: different action vocabularies and thresholds within one person, amendments that change a guard rather than just an action, partial retractions, local versus global exceptions, and uncertainty shared across projects. Longer names and less repeated predicates should exert real pressure on the whole-card budget. Answers should be checked against source commitments and an explicit reference before any new experiment is frozen. A new batch requires a separately reviewed finite authorization; this closed scope grants none.

The current observation supplies no evidence-backed encoding defect to patch. We preserve the successful source/card boundaries and the unresolved challenge instead of calling a synthetic damage control an observed loss or silently revising these sources after seeing results. No human, cross-provider, population or general-taste fidelity claim is made.
