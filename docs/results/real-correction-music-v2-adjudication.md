prediction addressed a different concern. The private clarification and model
response remain local; no new inference was performed.

This resolves the one ambiguous case in a separate post-hoc layer. All three
conditions now have **0/4** under that owner-informed adjudication. The original

The original score file's digest is bound in the separate JSON record.

The owner saw the proposed interpretation before clarifying, and the assistant
made the match judgment. This is not independent blind grading or a newly
preregistered experiment. Clarification also does not establish that the intended
objection was predictable from earlier history. Do not add it to an old request,
retry the prediction and describe the result as held out.

The case illustrates why a model's plausible use of history cannot by itself
establish that it understood the user's actual objection. It gives no basis for
a cross-domain preference rule or an automatic change to the exported prompt.
