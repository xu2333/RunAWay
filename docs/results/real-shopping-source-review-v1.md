# Source review restored both choices on the shorter card and lost one rate on the longer card

The reviewer compared each of two fixed drafts with the same prior conversation, object by object. It received neither the shopping task nor feedback about omissions. Each original and reviewed card then went to the same reader twice. All results below are fresh deliveries on the same previously measured task.

| Full credit in a delivery | 659 original | 756 reviewed | 690 original | 714 reviewed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Qualified television protection and ordinary choice | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Qualified tablet discount and ordinary choice | 0/2 | 2/2 | 1/2 | 2/2 |
| Both choices in the same plan | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Correct tablet percentage applied to its condition | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 0/2 |
| Named ordinary tablet seller and historical price | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Percentage and named ordinary price together | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 0/2 |
| All seven original task requirements | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 |

The **659-character parent** had omitted both benefits. The review recovered the conditional five-year protection and ordinary television seller, and the tablet's 5% cardholder discount and ordinary seller at 499. All appeared in the reviewed claims, the 756-character portable text and both deliveries. One delivery used the ordinary seller's 499 as the card retailer's list price when explaining the discount. The historical source gave 499.99 for the latter. The qualification, rate, historical discounted price and ordinary option were correct under the frozen rubric. The arithmetic explanation introduced a separate precision error.

The **690-character parent** already carried both tablet prices and the ordinary seller. Neither fresh original read connected that seller to its ordinary price. The review's detailed decision explicitly recovered 5%, but the 714-character text retained only the cardholder price and the named ordinary alternative. Both reviewed deliveries applied the ordinary seller and 499, and both missed the percentage. Recovering a fact in the evidence-backed decision helped locate where it disappeared a second time.

All eight deliveries retained the exact television designation and size, selected priced stand bundle, actionable route without the unavailable membership, conditional third-party cashback and a timed three-category plan. Both reviewed views yielded one of two plans explicitly stating the unavailable club membership with its purchase scope. The other plans could still use the ordinary route. No plan claimed to have verified current offers or assumed unknown owner eligibility. No baseline full-credit collateral decision fell on review. The seven-requirement joint score treats the tablet benefit as a usable choice; the separately frozen exact-percentage endpoint remains unmet on the longer lineage.

The same 690-character parent had done better on television in the [preceding round](real-shopping-branches-v1.md). The two fresh original reads mentioned protection but left the ordinary route generic. Fixed text still produced different levels of detail between reads. Each draft received one review, so this comparison provides no estimate of repeat-review stability or performance on new people or tasks.

All ten authorized attempts completed. The service reported 72,152 input tokens (including 8,448 cached) and 19,066 output tokens. Monetary cost was unavailable. A single analyst assessed shuffled deliveries before inspecting the assignments or reviewed cards. Results describe a historical planning replay.
